Coconut water is often called "Mother Nature's sports drink," and for good reason. It is naturally rich in electrolytes, particularly potassium, which is crucial for maintaining cellular hydration and preventing muscle cramps.

Unlike artificial sports drinks that are often loaded with refined sugars, artificial food dyes, and chemical preservatives, pure coconut water provides hydration straight from the source. It is naturally low in calories and completely fat-free.

## The Electrolyte Profile

A standard 500ml serving of fresh coconut water can contain more potassium than a banana. This makes it an ideal post-workout recovery drink. It also contains trace amounts of sodium, magnesium, and calcium.

- **Potassium**: Helps prevent cramps.
- **Magnesium**: Essential for muscle relaxation.
- **Calcium**: Important for bone health.

## Best Time to Drink

You can enjoy coconut water any time of the day. However, it is especially beneficial:
1. First thing in the morning on an empty stomach.
2. Immediately after a rigorous workout or a run.
3. On a hot summer day when you've been sweating heavily.

Next time you reach for hydration, consider the natural choice.
